The Single-Storey House is a family home laid out entirely on the ground floor, with a usable area of 99.5 m² and three bedrooms. Walls built from industrial hemp blocks, a roof terrace with a timber canopy and the 32.2 m² terrace define a compact, healthy and environmentally friendly way of living. The Technical Design documentation was produced by CONSARHOUSE in 2026.
