S+P+E House 2 is a single-family home with basement, ground and upper floor — approximately 252 m² of usable area on a 103.34 m² footprint — with a garage in the basement accessed by ramp, three bedrooms upstairs and a contemporary volumetric composition in white, anthracite and timber slats. The Technical Design documentation was produced by CONSARHOUSE in 2026.
