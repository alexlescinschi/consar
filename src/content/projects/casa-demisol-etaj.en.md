The House with Basement and Ground Floor is a single-family home (D+P scheme) designed on a sloping plot: the basement level with garage and technical spaces is embedded into the terrain, while the ground floor, with its living area opening onto the garden, rises above the slope under a flat roof. The Technical Design documentation was produced by CONSARHOUSE in 2026.
