The House with Basement, Ground and Upper Floor is a single-family home (S+P+E scheme) organised in two stacked volumetric registers: the white ground floor with a double garage and living areas, and the recessed anthracite upper floor with the night area and a roof terrace. The Technical Design documentation was produced by CONSARHOUSE in 2026.
